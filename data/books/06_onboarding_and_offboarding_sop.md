
## 1. Purpose and roles
This SOP coordinates joining and leaving Northstar. Employment terms and applicable law remain authoritative.

- **People Operations:** Maintains employee records and coordinates documentation.
- **Hiring manager:** Defines role expectations, assigns a buddy where appropriate, and prepares a first-week plan.
- **IT Service Desk:** Provisions and removes access from approved requests.
- **Workplace Operations:** Coordinates access cards, seating, and equipment.
- **Employee:** Completes required forms, protects credentials, and returns assets.

## 2. Pre-joining checklist
The hiring manager confirms role, reporting line, start date, work location, and essential tools. People Operations sends approved joining instructions and requests only required employment documentation.

IT provisioning must be based on an approved request specifying employee, manager, start date, role, applications, and access level. Grant only role-appropriate permissions; a new employee should not receive administrator access merely for convenience.

Workplace Operations confirms laptop, access card, peripherals, and office arrangements. The manager prepares a schedule with team introductions, security training, role context, and early deliverables.

## 3. First day
1. Confirm access to the company identity platform.
2. Set up multi-factor authentication.
3. Confirm device enrollment in required management and security tools.
4. Review the employee handbook and information-security policy.
5. Explain the HR portal, IT Service Desk, knowledge base, and communication channels.
6. Introduce the manager, buddy, and key collaborators.
7. Review first-week goals and questions.

Never ask an employee to send a password or MFA code to a manager or IT colleague. Use approved identity-recovery procedures.

## 4. First 30 days
The manager clarifies role outcomes, decision rights, stakeholders, and performance discussions. The employee completes mandatory training and learns approved tools and workflows. Missing access should be reported through the Service Desk rather than solved by borrowing credentials.

At the end of the first month, the manager and employee discuss progress, unclear areas, and support needed.

## 5. Role changes
When responsibilities change, the manager requests access changes. Existing permissions should be reviewed and removed when no longer needed. A new role should not automatically inherit every permission from the previous role. The transfer is not complete from a security perspective until access has been reviewed.

## 6. Offboarding initiation
When departure is confirmed, People Operations coordinates the plan with the manager and relevant teams. Access-removal timing follows the approved plan and applicable law. For involuntary exits or elevated risk, People Operations and Security may require coordinated, time-sensitive actions. Share departure details only with people who need them.

## 7. Offboarding checklist
- Confirm final working date and responsible manager.
- Identify systems, shared resources, customer responsibilities, and ongoing work.
- Schedule access removal with IT and system owners.
- Collect company equipment, access cards, and keys.
- Transfer ownership of business files through approved methods.
- Revoke tokens, keys, and external access where applicable.
- Complete expense, asset, and administrative checks.
- Remind the departing employee of continuing confidentiality obligations.
- Record completion and outstanding actions.

Managers must not ask departing employees to share personal passwords. Transfer business accounts through authorized administrative processes.

## 8. Equipment and knowledge transfer
Return equipment by the date communicated by People Operations or Workplace Operations. For remote employees, follow the approved shipping or collection process. Report damaged or missing equipment honestly.

The manager identifies recurring tasks, open projects, contacts, deadlines, and documentation for handover. Do not copy confidential information to personal accounts or uncontrolled storage. Reassign customer communications to an authorized owner.

## 9. Exceptions and urgent cases
If a start date changes, notify People Operations, the manager, and IT. If an employee leaves unexpectedly or a security concern arises, contact People Operations and Information Security immediately. Do not delay a necessary security action while waiting for a routine checklist to be completed.

## 10. Completion records
Each team records completion in the approved workflow. If an item remains incomplete, record its owner, reason, risk, and expected resolution date. An unchecked item must not be treated as complete simply because the employee’s final date has passed.

## 11. Document control
Owner: Operations. Review annually or when identity, HR, or asset-management processes change.
