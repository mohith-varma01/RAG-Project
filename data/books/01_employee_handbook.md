

## 1. Purpose and scope
This handbook explains everyday people policies at Northstar Digital Services Pvt. Ltd. (“Northstar”). It applies to full-time employees in India and should be read with employment letters and more specific policies. Where a signed employment agreement or later formally approved policy conflicts with this handbook, contact People Operations for clarification.

## 2. Working principles
Employees are expected to act with integrity, communicate respectfully, protect company and customer information, and raise concerns early. Managers should explain team expectations and apply decisions consistently. Ask a manager or People Operations when a rule is unclear rather than relying on informal assumptions.

## 3. Working hours and hybrid work
The standard workweek is Monday through Friday. Most teams operate between 9:30 a.m. and 6:30 p.m. India Standard Time, including a reasonable meal break. Customer-facing and operational teams may have different schedules approved by their department head.

The default hybrid-work expectation is three office days per week for roles where hybrid work is suitable. Teams may establish anchor days. Employees should confirm their team’s schedule with their manager. Remote work does not change expectations for availability, security, or delivery.

Temporary changes should be discussed with the manager. Long-term exceptions require written approval from the department head and People Operations. A verbal agreement should not be treated as a permanent policy change.

## 4. Attendance
Employees should join meetings on time and notify their manager if they expect to be late or unavailable. For an unexpected absence, inform the manager as early as practical and record the absence in the HR portal when able. Repeated unexplained absences may be reviewed by the manager and People Operations.

## 5. Leave
Leave requests and balances are managed in the HR portal. A request is not approved merely because it has been submitted; wait until its status changes to “Approved.” The detailed rules for annual leave, sick leave, and other absences are in **Leave and Benefits Policy (HR-POL-002)**, the primary reference for leave entitlements and procedures.

## 6. Respectful workplace
Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, retaliation, and deliberate exclusion are not acceptable. Concerns may be raised with a manager, People Operations, or the designated workplace-concerns channel. Employees may bypass their manager if the manager is involved or they do not feel comfortable raising the concern with them.

Reports are handled as discreetly as practical while allowing a fair review. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith or participates in a review is prohibited.

## 7. Conflicts of interest
Employees must disclose situations that could reasonably create a conflict between personal interests and Northstar’s interests. Examples include evaluating a supplier owned by a family member, holding a significant financial interest in a competitor, or accepting outside work that interferes with company responsibilities. Disclosures should be sent to the manager and Compliance. Disclosure does not automatically mean an activity is prohibited; Compliance assesses the circumstances.

## 8. Company property and confidentiality
Company laptops, access cards, software accounts, and other equipment must be kept secure and returned when requested or when employment ends. Lost equipment or suspected unauthorized access must be reported promptly to IT Service Desk and the manager.

Internal plans, pricing, customer records, product roadmaps, financial information, source code, and employee data must be shared only with people who have a legitimate business need and appropriate access. Do not forward confidential material to personal email or upload it to unapproved external services. Confidentiality obligations continue after employment ends.

## 9. Performance and development
Managers should set clear goals, provide feedback throughout the year, and discuss development needs. Formal review cycles are announced by People Operations. Performance should consider outcomes, quality, collaboration, customer impact, and role-specific expectations—not simply hours worked or message volume.

Employees may access approved learning resources and apply for internal opportunities when they meet the stated requirements. Some learning programmes require manager approval because of cost or workload.

## 10. Where to ask questions
Contact People Operations through the HR portal for handbook questions, IT Service Desk for security or device issues, and Compliance for ethical concerns. This handbook is guidance and does not replace a signed employment agreement or a more specific policy.

## 11. Document control
Owner: People Operations. Review annually or when material business or legal changes occur.
