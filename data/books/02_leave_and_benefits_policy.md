
## 1. Purpose
This policy describes leave categories, request procedures, and selected benefits for Northstar employees in India. Employment terms and applicable law may provide additional rights. Contact People Operations if your employment letter contains a more specific entitlement.

## 2. Annual leave
Eligible full-time employees receive **18 working days of annual leave per leave year**. Check the HR portal for your current balance. Submit planned annual leave at least **five working days before** the first day. Requests for five or more consecutive working days should normally be submitted at least **ten working days in advance**. These are planning expectations, not guarantees of approval.

Managers consider project deadlines, team coverage, and fairness. Do not book non-refundable travel until leave is approved. If business needs require a change to approved leave, the manager should discuss the reason with the employee and involve People Operations where appropriate.

Up to **5 unused annual-leave days** may be carried into the next leave year. Amounts above the limit are handled under the company’s year-end process and applicable law. Ask People Operations rather than assuming excess days will be paid out.

## 3. Sick leave
Eligible employees receive **12 working days of sick leave per leave year**. Notify your manager as soon as reasonably possible when illness prevents work. For an absence of **three or more consecutive working days**, People Operations may request reasonable supporting documentation, subject to privacy requirements. Record the absence in the HR portal when practical.

Unused sick leave does not carry forward and is not converted into cash under this policy.

## 4. Holidays and statutory leave
Northstar publishes a holiday calendar for each office location. Customer operations teams may have approved holiday coverage schedules; the manager explains applicable arrangements.

Maternity-related benefits, parental leave, adoption leave, and other statutory entitlements are administered under applicable law and employee eligibility. Contact People Operations before planning such leave because documentation and eligibility may depend on individual circumstances.

## 5. Bereavement and emergency leave
Employees facing a serious family emergency should contact their manager and People Operations as soon as practical. The company will assess the circumstances, available balance, and legal requirements. An informal conversation does not automatically record or approve leave; update the HR portal when possible.

## 6. Request workflow
1. Open the HR portal and select **Leave & Attendance**.
2. Choose the leave category and dates.
3. Add a short note where required; do not include unnecessary medical details in general comments.
4. Submit the request to the assigned manager.
5. Monitor the status until it shows **Approved** or **Declined**.
6. If urgent and the manager is unavailable, contact the designated backup or People Operations.

A submitted request is pending, not approved.

## 7. Benefits
Eligible employees may have group medical insurance, an employee assistance programme, learning support, and approved wellness initiatives. Coverage, dependent eligibility, limits, and exclusions are described in the current benefits portal and insurer documents. The benefits portal is the authoritative source for current coverage.

A service is not automatically reimbursable because it is described as a wellness or learning expense. Confirm eligibility and approval requirements before spending.

## 8. Examples
**Example A:** An employee requests four days of annual leave eight working days in advance. The request meets the standard notice period for a short absence, but approval still depends on coverage and manager review.

**Example B:** An employee requests leave for tomorrow but has no approval yet. The request remains pending; contact the manager or backup rather than assuming approval.

**Example C:** An employee is sick for four consecutive working days. Notify the manager, record the absence, and respond to any reasonable documentation request.

## 9. Questions and corrections
For a missing or incorrect balance, raise a People Operations ticket with dates and leave type. Do not send medical records through a public team channel. Ask for a written explanation if eligibility is disputed.
