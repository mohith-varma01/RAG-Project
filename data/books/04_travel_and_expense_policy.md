
## 1. Purpose
This policy explains approval, booking, and reimbursement for business travel and expenses in India. Expenses must have a legitimate business purpose and supporting documentation.

## 2. Approval before booking
Obtain written manager approval before making a business travel booking. Include purpose, destination, dates, expected cost, customer or event details where relevant, and project or cost centre. International travel also requires department-head and Finance approval. A calendar invitation or verbal discussion is not formal approval.

## 3. Booking channels
Use the company-approved travel portal or designated travel desk. If the portal cannot meet a documented business need, contact Finance before booking directly. Select a reasonable itinerary balancing cost, travel time, safety, and meeting requirements.

Personal upgrades and leisure nights are at the employee’s expense unless a written exception is approved. When combining personal and business travel, provide a cost comparison and claim only the business-related portion.

## 4. Air and rail
Domestic air travel should generally be economy class. Exceptions require written department-head approval before booking. Rail class should be appropriate to journey duration, safety, and company guidance. Do not split bookings or misrepresent an itinerary to avoid approval limits.

## 5. Hotels
Choose a safe, business-appropriate hotel within the approved budget and reasonably close to the meeting location. If the preferred option exceeds budget because of a major event, limited availability, or safety concerns, obtain approval before confirming. Itemized invoices are required.

Personal minibar purchases, entertainment, in-room movies, and accompanying family members’ costs are not reimbursable unless a written exception applies.

## 6. Local transport
Use reasonable transport for the destination and timing. Claims should state date, route, business purpose, and amount. Attach ride-hailing receipts or other available receipts. Personal vehicle claims must follow the mileage method published by Finance; do not invent a per-kilometre rate.

## 7. Meals and hospitality
Reasonable meals during approved business travel may be reimbursed within current limits in the Finance portal. Alcohol is not reimbursable under the standard employee travel policy. Customer entertainment requires advance manager approval and must comply with the Gifts and Hospitality Standard. Do not claim meals already paid for by a conference, hotel package, customer, or another employee.

## 8. Non-reimbursable expenses
Examples include personal shopping, leisure activities, traffic or parking fines, avoidable personal itinerary changes, duplicate claims, and expenses without a valid business purpose. Ask Finance before submitting an unclear item.

## 9. Claim process and deadline
1. Open the Finance expense portal.
2. Create a claim and select the trip or business purpose.
3. Enter each expense separately with date, category, amount, currency, and justification.
4. Attach itemized receipts and required approvals.
5. Submit to the assigned manager.
6. Respond to questions from the manager or Finance.
7. Track the claim until approved and processed.

Claims should normally be submitted within **30 calendar days after the trip ends**. If a delay is unavoidable, explain the reason and contact Finance. Submission within 30 days does not guarantee approval.

## 10. Currency and missing receipts
For foreign-currency expenses, provide the original amount and currency plus a card statement or other accepted evidence of the INR amount charged. Finance applies the company’s designated conversion method.

If a receipt is unavailable, provide the date, merchant, amount, business purpose, and reason it is missing. Finance may request evidence or reject an inadequately substantiated claim. A missing-receipt statement does not guarantee reimbursement.

## 11. Cancellations and examples
Notify the travel desk and manager promptly if a trip is cancelled or changed. Refunds and ticket credits must be returned to the company or recorded as Finance directs; employees must not keep unused credits for personal travel.

**Example A:** A manager approves a domestic trip, but the employee books a hotel above budget. Approval for the trip does not automatically approve the excess hotel cost.

**Example B:** A claim submitted 20 days after returning meets the normal submission timeline but remains subject to review.

**Example C:** A taxi receipt is missing. Provide the required details; reimbursement is not guaranteed.

## 12. Document control
Owner: Finance Operations. Current reimbursement limits and portal workflows are maintained in the Finance portal and may be updated separately.
